## Personal statement

I am a clinical academic psychiatrist whose work sits at the interface between molecular diagnostics, clinical trials, and the redesign of NHS services for early diagnosis of neurodegenerative disease. My distinctive contribution has been to translate emerging biomarker science into deliverable NHS pathways: in 2022 I established the UK's first psychiatrist-led CSF biomarker service, initially single-handed, and scaled it into the Manchester Brain Health Centre — now underpinned by philanthropic and third-sector partnerships and £35 million of industry investment, and serving as rolling research infrastructure. I lead the most active dementia research centre in the North West, am Principal or Co-Investigator on more than 30 clinical trials spanning Phase I–III, and combine this delivery focus with formal training in statistics and computation. My current priorities are platform trials for disease-modifying therapies, molecular and imaging diagnostics, and the vascular contribution to cognitive impairment.

## 1. Generation of knowledge

My research contributions span clinical trials, biomarker science, and methodology. As Clinical Director of the Greater Manchester Dementia Research Centre I have been Principal or Co-Investigator on a portfolio exceeding £15 million, including the Alzheimer's monoclonal antibody programmes (Aducanumab, Gantenerumab, AL002), tau-targeting and metabolic therapeutics, and platform trials. I am a Co-Investigator and Outcomes Work-Package lead on **AD-SMART**, the UK's multi-arm platform trial for Alzheimer's disease (MRC/DRI/ARUK, £7.5M), and Site PI on **READ-OUT**, a £4.5M real-world dementia outcomes programme.

My published work reflects three threads. First, biomarkers and diagnostic pathways: I have built the evidence base for in-house NHS CSF diagnostics and brain health clinics, and contributed to the design of the AD-SMART drug-repurposing prioritisation pipeline (*Alzheimer's & Dementia*, 2026). Second, the vascular and systemic contribution to cognition, including mechanistic work on cerebral hypoperfusion in hypertensive vascular dementia and a series of collaborative studies on cognitive impairment in chronic kidney disease. Third, my earlier programme on the neurobiology of electroconvulsive therapy, including a meta-analysis of electrode placement that continues to inform practice. Across these I bring quantitative rigour — a Postgraduate Diploma in Statistics, current BSc in Mathematics, and applied skills in mixed-effects modelling and machine learning.

## 2. Development of individuals

I am a registered trainer and educational supervisor for higher trainees in old age psychiatry and a former faculty member for the Neurology Academy in Sheffield, teaching on Parkinson's neuropsychiatry, dementia and mild cognitive impairment. I teach undergraduates at the University of Manchester and have previously taught at Trinity College Dublin and Cambridge.

Beyond formal training, I supervise and mentor across disciplines. I currently support University of Manchester collaborators and fellows in cardiovascular science, vision science, neuroimaging and hearing health, including doctoral and fellowship applications on novel biomarkers of neurodegeneration. As the first UK psychiatrist to establish a CSF biomarker clinic, I have mentored clinicians across the UK, Middle East and Europe in replicating the service, and have hosted visiting fellows from Spain and Oman. Growing the next generation of biomarker-literate clinicians is, to my mind, as important as any single trial result, because it is what allows new diagnostics to reach patients at scale.

## 3. Contributions to the wider research community

I co-founded and co-chair the **UK Brain Health Coalition**, convened under Alzheimer's Research UK and the Alzheimer's Society, through which I lead national dissemination of early-diagnosis and post-diagnostic models. I served as Clinical Research Network / RDN Dementia Lead for Greater Manchester and now for the North West of England, facilitating trial delivery across the region, and I am a member of the national Dementia Trials Network.

I give substantial time to peer and grant review: I co-chaired Alzheimer's Society UK's grant review board (2022–25), and have served on review boards for Alzheimer's Research UK, the Alzheimer Society of Ireland, and the NIHR i4i programme. I chair the COBALT trial steering committee (NIHR EME) and sit on a second, and I was invited guest editor for the *British Journal of Psychiatry*'s special issue on disease-modifying therapies in Alzheimer's disease. During the COVID-19 pandemic I contributed to NIHR's working group on remote, COVID-safe trial delivery. This community work increasingly extends to industry: alongside DPUK and Lilly I have led multi-site NHS evaluations of CSF service feasibility and of system readiness for disease-modifying therapies, helping the wider system prepare for new treatments.

## 4. Contributions to broader society

My central societal contribution is the transformation of how neurodegenerative disease is diagnosed in the NHS. Establishing England's CSF biomarker capability — and helping grow the number of biomarker-active brain health clinics from one to ten in two years — has changed the diagnostic offer available to patients well beyond Manchester, with my laboratory now receiving samples from Sussex, Oxford, Sheffield, Salford and Surrey. This work has been recognised by three Health Service Journal Awards (Modernising Diagnostics, 2023; Best NHS Collaboration and Best Pharmaceutical Partnership, 2025) and a national Clinical Impact Award.

I have convened the regional multidisciplinary team preparing for the safe introduction of monoclonal antibody therapies, bringing together neurology, neuroradiology, nuclear medicine, geriatrics and pharmacy. I am a regular and trusted voice for patients and the public — speaking to the Trafford MCI group, Together Dementia Support, and Parkinson's peer-support groups, and contributing to public media including BBC Radio Manchester. I hold no shares or financial interest in any pharmaceutical company; where I advise industry, all income is contracted through my NHS Trust and reinvested in research and innovation. My motivation throughout is to ensure that the scientific advances now reaching the clinic are delivered equitably, safely and at scale within a public health system.
